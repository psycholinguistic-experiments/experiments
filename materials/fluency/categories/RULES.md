# Category rules for the fluency task's word lists

Two lists, in simplified Chinese, one word per line: `animals.txt` and
`fruitveg.txt`. A student's answer counts when it is in SUBTLEX-CH **and** in
the list for the block it was typed in (an animal typed in the fruit and
vegetable block does not count there).

## Animals — include

- Any real animal, named the way people name it: mammals, birds, fish,
  reptiles, amphibians, insects, spiders, worms, molluscs, crustaceans and
  other sea creatures (猫, 熊猫, 金鱼, 蚂蚁, 海星, 水母, 珊瑚).
- Basic kind words that are themselves animal names: 鸟, 鱼, 虫, 昆虫, 蛇, 鸟儿.
- Breeds and varieties (哈士奇, 金毛, 波斯猫, 藏獒, 锦鲤), and extinct animals
  (恐龙, 霸王龙, 猛犸象, 剑齿虎, 渡渡鸟).
- Young, sex, pet and diminutive forms (小猫, 小狗, 猫咪, 狗狗, 母鸡, 公鸡,
  牛犊, 羊羔, 小鸡), and common alternative or colloquial names (老虎/虎,
  耗子, 马骝, 刺猬).

## Animals — exclude

- Group or class labels: 动物, 宠物, 家禽, 牲畜, 野兽, 猛兽, 害虫, 哺乳动物,
  爬行动物, 鸟类, 鱼类, 猫科动物, any word ending in 类 or 动物.
- Mythical and fictional beings: 龙, 凤凰, 麒麟, 独角兽, 美人鱼, 貔貅
  (but real animals named with 龙 count: 恐龙, 科莫多龙).
- People and roles (人, 猎人, 牛仔), animal foods, products and body parts
  (牛肉, 鸡蛋, 鱼翅, 猪蹄, 牛奶), dishes (烤鸭), names of characters, teams or
  brands, and words that only use an animal figuratively (狗屁, 熊孩子, 菜鸟,
  牛逼).

## Fruits and vegetables — include

- Fruits: berries, melons, citrus, stone fruit, tropical fruit, and their
  varieties (苹果, 红富士, 哈密瓜, 西瓜, 柠檬, 榴莲, 火龙果).
- Vegetables: leaf, stem, root, bulb, flower vegetables, gourds, peppers
  (白菜, 青菜, 菠菜, 芹菜, 胡萝卜, 洋葱, 西兰花, 黄瓜, 南瓜, 辣椒, 青椒).
- Mushrooms and other edible fungi (蘑菇, 香菇, 金针菇, 木耳).
- Beans and peas eaten as vegetables or as beans (豌豆, 毛豆, 豆角, 四季豆,
  黄豆, 绿豆, 红豆), sprouts (豆芽).
- Tubers and starchy roots (土豆, 马铃薯, 红薯, 地瓜, 芋头, 山药), corn (玉米).
- Aromatics eaten as vegetables (葱, 大葱, 姜, 蒜, 大蒜, 香菜, 韭菜).
- Nuts and their kernels (核桃, 花生, 栗子, 板栗, 杏仁, 腰果, 榛子, 开心果).
- Dried and preserved forms that are still the fruit itself (红枣, 葡萄干,
  桂圆, 柿饼).

## Fruits and vegetables — exclude

- Group labels: 水果, 蔬菜, 果蔬, 果实, 干果, 野菜, 坚果.
- Dishes, drinks and processed foods: 果汁, 果酱, 果冻, 泡菜, 咸菜, 薯条,
  薯片, 番茄酱, 豆腐, 豆浆, 沙拉.
- Grains and flours: 米, 大米, 小米, 小麦, 面粉 (but 玉米 counts).
- Spices that are not eaten as vegetables: 胡椒, 花椒, 八角, 桂皮, 孜然.
- Flowers and plants that are not eaten (玫瑰, 树, 草), seeds sold as snacks
  (瓜子), and figurative uses (傻瓜, 菜鸟, 菜单, 豆腐渣).

When in doubt, include a word only if a native speaker naming animals (or
fruits and vegetables) in a test would be credited for it.
