In-class experiments

Three short experiments. Please do them in this order, one after the other.

  1. 1

    Word task 1

    Decide, as fast as you can, whether letter strings are English words. About 5 minutes · keyboard

    Start →
  2. 2

    Word task 2

    The same decision, with a twist you will see straight away. About 5 minutes · keyboard

    Start →
  3. 3

    Shape task

    Match made-up words to shapes. No right or wrong answers. About 3 minutes · keyboard or touch

    Start →

Why this order?

Word task 1 only works if you don't know what to look for. Doing Word task 2 first would give it away.

Your data

Everything is anonymous. Each device gets a random code, and no names or student numbers are collected. The class results are shown together on the projector.

For the best timing

Use a laptop with a keyboard, close other tabs, plug in if your battery is low, and don't switch windows during a task.
